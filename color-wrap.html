Color change wrap

Quick answer

A full color change wrap in Scottsdale starts at $3,200 and can reach $5,500 depending on the vehicle. Most full wraps take 3–4 days. Chrome delete starts at $450.

Why it's worth it

Key benefits.

  • Reversible: the vinyl can be removed when you want your original color back.
  • Paint-friendly: it covers factory paint instead of replacing it.
  • Hundreds of looks: choose matte, satin, gloss or a custom color.
  • Chrome delete: turn shiny trim into gloss or satin black.

02How long do you need my car?

Plan on 3–4 days for a full wrap, because the car is washed, decontaminated, wrapped and left to cure.

03Can I wash my car after it's wrapped?
