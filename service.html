FAQ

About paint protection film.

01Can you really not see it?

On gloss paint it's invisible from a normal distance. With wrapped edges, even up close.

02Can I wash my car normally?

Yes, after 7 days. Hand wash or touchless; avoid brushes for the first month.

03Can I combine it with ceramic coating?

Yes, and we recommend it: ceramic on top of the film makes cleaning even easier.
