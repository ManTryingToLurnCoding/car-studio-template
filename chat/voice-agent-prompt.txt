## Service explanations

- PPF is a clear, self-healing film that helps protect paint from rock chips, light scratches and road debris.
- A full front package covers the front bumper, full hood, front fenders and side mirrors.
- A color change wrap is vinyl that changes the car's color and can be removed later.
- Chrome delete covers shiny trim with gloss or satin black.
- Ceramic coating adds gloss and helps water and grime release more easily. It can go over paint or PPF.
- Ceramic tint helps block heat and UV.
