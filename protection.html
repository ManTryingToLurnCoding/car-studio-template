Behind the system

How this system is protected.

A lead system handles customer details and runs on paid tools. These protections keep spam out, keep real requests safe, and leave judgment calls to your team.

Spam stays out

Forms use a Cloudflare bot check that is verified on the server, plus a hidden trap field for simple bots.

Floods get stopped

Requests are limited per visitor, per phone number and per day, so an attack can't burn through your automation budget.

No lead is thrown away

Only exact repeats are blocked. A second car or a corrected request still reaches the CRM, and your team decides what to keep.

Bad data gets rejected

The server checks every field, service and budget. Links and markup can't be slipped into names or text messages.

Private keys stay private

CRM and automation keys live on the server, never in the website code. Rate-limit records store scrambled values instead of phone numbers.

AI has limited access

The chat and voice agents can only submit a lead through a protected route. They can't read the CRM or choose a deal's stage.

Before a real launch

  • Privacy and consent: add a privacy policy and complete the SMS registration required for real texting.
  • Opt-outs: connect STOP replies, so anyone who opts out stays opted out.
  • Access: move every account to the client, with new keys and two-factor sign-in.
  • Daily habit: the team reviews new leads and handles repeat requests from the same phone number.

No online system can be guaranteed unbreakable. The goal is to reduce the risk, limit the damage, and make problems easy to spot. Texts on this demo are simulated.
