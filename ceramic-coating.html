Ceramic coating

Deep shine, easier washes. A liquid glass-like layer that helps water, mud and grime slide off.

Quick answer

Ceramic coating in Scottsdale costs $900 to $1,800 depending on the package and vehicle. Most coatings are completed in 1 day and can be applied over paint or PPF.

Why it's worth it

Key benefits.

  • Deep gloss: it sharpens the color and shine of the paint.
  • Water beading: water and dirt release more easily.
  • Easier cleaning: routine washes take less effort.
  • Works with PPF: add it over film for easier upkeep.
