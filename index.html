FAQ

Everything you may want to know.

Still unsure? Call us, we answer in person.

01How long does my car stay with you?

4 to 7 days for a full wrap or PPF, 1 to 2 days for ceramic. You get photos and videos every day.

[F1]Car in workshop
02Will the film damage my original paint?

No. Films are removable and protect the paint underneath. Removal leaves the factory finish intact.

03What warranty do I get?

2 years on installation, up to 10 years on paint protection film from the manufacturer.

04Is window tint legal?

Yes, within the legal light transmission limits. We tell you exactly what's allowed for your car.

05I'm not in your city. Can you still do it?

Yes. We collect and deliver your car in a closed trailer, nationwide and across Europe.
