FAQ

Quick answers.

01How much does a full wrap or PPF cost?

Color change wraps start at $3,200. Full front PPF starts at $1,800, and full-body PPF starts at $6,000. A 15-minute in-person inspection confirms the exact price.

02How long do you need my car?

Tint and ceramic coating take about 1 day. Full wraps and full-body PPF take 3–4 days.

03Can I wash my car after it's wrapped?

Wait 7 days after installation, then hand wash only.
