Paint protection film

Quick answer

Paint protection film (PPF) in Scottsdale starts at $1,800 for a full front package and $6,000 for a full body. A full-body install usually takes 3–4 days.

Why it's worth it

Key benefits.

  • Nearly invisible: the clear film keeps the look of your paint.
  • Self-healing: light scratches fade with heat or sun.
  • Rock chip protection: it takes road debris before your paint does.
  • Better resale: the factory paint stays protected underneath.

FAQ

About paint protection film.

01How much does PPF cost?

A full front package starts at $1,800. Full-body PPF starts at $6,000. An in-person inspection confirms the exact price.

02What is a full front package?

It's the most popular PPF option. It covers the front bumper, full hood, front fenders and side mirrors, where most rock hits land.

03How long do you need my car?

About 2 days for a full front and 3–4 days for full-body PPF.

04Can I wash my car after PPF?

Wait 7 days, then hand wash only.
